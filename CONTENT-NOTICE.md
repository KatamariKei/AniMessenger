# Content notice

AniMessenger is an unofficial, user-operated character-messaging project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the creators, publishers, studios, game companies, catalogue providers, or rights holders associated with characters found through public research sources.

Character names, likenesses, source artwork, franchises, and other third-party material remain the property of their respective owners. The repository does not include commercial model weights, third-party catalogue artwork or datasets, generated user galleries, or private conversations.

## Generated profiles and conversations

Character profiles and messages are generated by locally selected language models using catalogue tags, limited public research, user edits, conversation context, and memory. They may be inaccurate, inconsistent, out of character, or fabricated. They are creative simulation, not authoritative canon or statements from a character's rights holder.

AniMessenger normalizes generated character profiles to an adult age of at least 18. This product rule does not change a character's original canon and should not be interpreted as source information.

## Generated images

Image generation is optional and runs through a user-configured ComfyUI workflow. Outputs depend on the models, LoRAs, prompts, settings, and source material installed by the user. Users are responsible for ensuring that their models, assets, inputs, outputs, and uses comply with applicable licenses, laws, and platform rules.

## Responsible use

Do not use AniMessenger to impersonate real people, misrepresent generated material as official, violate another person's privacy, or distribute content you do not have the right to share. Public contributors should use sanitized examples instead of private conversations or generated galleries.
